About Me

I'm a Junior Full-Stack Developer with a background in visual arts and a passion for creating elegant, accessible and user-focused digital experiences. My Journey combines creativity, problem-solving and a growing technical skill set in web development and IT Support.

Read More

After completing my Full-Stack Web Development training at Code Factory Vienna, I have been building responsive interfaces, experimenting with interactive UI animations and strengthening my understanding of modern frontend and backend technologies.

I enjoy transforming ideas into clean and maintainable solutions, paying attention not only to functionality but also to visual harmony, usability and detail. My artistic background helps me approach development with a strong sense of composition, color and user experience.
